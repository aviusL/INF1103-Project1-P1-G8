
# AI Zero-Waste Kitchen: Household Food Waste Reduction

A smart cooking assistant designed to match leftover ingredients with safe, allergen-free recipes—minimizing household food waste through dynamic AI generation and deterministic safety verification.

---

## Project Overview

Students and home cooks frequently allow food to spoil because they lack the culinary knowledge to combine random leftovers into cohesive meals. Furthermore, individuals with food allergies face serious health risks when improvising dishes without strict dietary safeguards. Existing platforms primarily cater to pre-planned grocery shopping rather than dynamic, on-hand inventory cooking.

**AI Zero-Waste Kitchen** solves this by accepting current fridge inventory, priority expiry dates, allergen constraints, and available cooking equipment to generate safe, optimized, and low-waste meal options.

---

## Coming Features

* **Expiry-Driven Prioritization:** Factors in ingredient expiration dates to maximize the use of items near their end-of-life.
* **Dynamic AI Generation:** Produces custom, structured recipe instructions tailored strictly to on-hand equipment and dietary preferences.
* **Deterministic Safety Verifier:** Rule-based logic engine that screens out user-specified allergens, bypassing AI hallucinations to protect health safety.
* **Smart Shopping Suggestions:** Identifies minor missing non-essential items (1–2 items) or advises purchasing key staples when inventory is insufficient.
* **Static Fallback System:** Serves pre-verified recipes automatically if AI confidence is low or API connections fail.
